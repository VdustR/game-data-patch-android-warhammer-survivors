## Generation prompt

Create a wide 1200:630 social sharing image for an unofficial Warhammer Survivors Android save patcher. Use the supplied app icon as the character and palette reference: blue-armored helmeted space marine left and gold-and-green fantasy champion right, divided by a jagged pale-blue lightning seam. Reimagine as deliberate 16-bit pixel art on dark gothic stone and brass. Crisp square pixel clusters, restricted ramps, hard edges, no blur or smooth gradients. Portraits occupy outer thirds and face inward. Gold pixel coins below center. Central dark stone field with exact text "WARHAMMER SURVIVORS" in large ivory pixel lettering and "ANDROID SAVE PATCHER" in smaller gold pixel lettering. No fake UI buttons, watermark or extra branding. Thin stepped brass frame. Important faces and text within central 80%. Strong authentic pixel-art identity, dramatic but restrained, readable at thumbnail size. Opaque background.
